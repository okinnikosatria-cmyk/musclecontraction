Physiological Controls

Current Cycle Phase

1. Resting State (Relaxation)

Tropomyosin blocks Myosin binding sites on Actin filaments. Calcium ions are safely stored in the Sarcoplasmic Reticulum (SR). Myosin heads are cocked with ADP + Pi.

Summary of the 4 Contraction Steps

1
Action Potential & Ca2+

Nerve impulse signals SR to release Calcium ions (Ca2+) into the sarcoplasm.

2
Troponin Binding

Ca2+ binds Troponin, shifting Tropomyosin away to expose Actin binding sites.

3
Cross-Bridge & Power Stroke

Myosin heads bind Actin and pivot (Power Stroke), pulling thin filaments toward the M-line.

4
ATP Detachment & Relaxation

New ATP binds Myosin head to detach cross-bridge. Ca2+ pumped back into SR for relaxation.
